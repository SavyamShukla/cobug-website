Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Subscription Services Overview

COBUG offers recurring digital software subscriptions:

  • COBUG Plus: ₹1 per month (Deep Code Context inspection in Chat AI).
  • COBUG Pro: ₹2 per month (AI on Code Editor with Antigravity mode and multi-file editing).

Subscriptions are billed monthly through our payment partner, Razorpay.

2. How to Cancel Your Subscription

You can cancel your active COBUG subscription at any time without penalty or cancellation fees:

Option 1 (Inside the App): Open COBUG Desktop → Click Settings → Navigate to AI Integration → Select Manage Plan → Click Cancel Subscription.

Option 2 (Via Email): Send an email to cobugapp@gmail.com from your registered account email with the subject "Cancel Subscription".

Upon cancellation, your subscription will not renew at the next billing cycle. You will continue to have full access to your paid features (Plus or Pro) until the end of your current 30-day paid billing cycle.

3. 7-Day Refund Policy

We want you to be completely confident using COBUG. We offer a 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee:

  • If you experience technical issues, payment errors, duplicate billing, or are unsatisfied with the service, you can request a 100% full refund within 7 days of the initial purchase or renewal.
  • To request a refund, simply email cobugapp@gmail.com or message us on Instagram at @cobug_app with your registered email and Razorpay payment ID.
  • Refund requests are verified and approved within 24 to 48 hours of receipt.

4. Refund Processing & Settlement Timeline

⏱️ Razorpay Refund Settlement Timeline

Once a refund is initiated and approved by COBUG, the transaction is processed through Razorpay back to your original source of payment:

  • UPI Payments: Refund typically reflects in your bank account within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Credit / Debit Cards & Net Banking: Refund takes 5 to 7 business days to reflect in your bank account or card statement, depending on your bank's clearance cycles.
