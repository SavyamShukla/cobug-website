DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Commitment to Your Privacy

At COBUG ("we", "us", or "our"), founded and developed by Savyam Shukla, we hold software developers' privacy and source code security to the highest standard. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how we protect your personal and codebase data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

  • Account Registration Data: Name, email address, username, and securely salted/hashed passwords when creating an account.
  • Authentication Information: Secure JWT tokens stored in your client application to maintain your logged-in session.
  • User Configuration: Your chosen subscription tier (Go, Plus, Pro) and preferences.
  • Bring-Your-Own API Keys: If you input external AI keys (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, or Ollama), these keys are stored securely on your local device / client session and transmitted only directly to the relevant provider.

3. Codebase Confidentiality & AI Privacy Guarantee

🔒 Absolute Code Privacy Guarantee

Your source code stays in your local machine's workspaces. COBUG does not upload, copy, or retain your private repository files on external servers.

We NEVER use your source code, prompts, or project files to train public AI models.

4. Payment Security & Razorpay

All financial transactions for COBUG Plus and COBUG Pro are handled by Razorpay Software Private Limited, an RBI-authorized and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment aggregator.

  • When you pay for a plan, your payment information (such as UPI ID, Credit/Debit card number, CVV, or NetBanking credentials) is transmitted directly to Razorpay's encrypted servers.
  • COBUG never sees, stores, or processes your credit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking PINs.
  • We only receive a cryptographic transaction token, order ID, and payment status verification from Razorpay to activate your plan.

5. Third-Party Services & AI Providers

When using external AI models (Gemini, ChatGPT, Groq, Ollama), code excerpts or prompts sent by you are processed according to the respective provider's developer data privacy policies. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of the AI model providers they connect.

6. Data Retention & User Rights

You retain full rights to your personal data:

  • Access & Export: You can request a summary of your account information at any time.
  • Correction: You may update your profile details directly from the COBUG settings page.
  • Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account and associated server records by emailing us at cobugapp@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 5 business days.

7. Security Measures

We implement standard 256-bit SSL encryption, token-based API authentication, and isolated database architectures to safeguard your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
